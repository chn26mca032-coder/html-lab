DIWALI - Festival of Light

Diwali is known as the festival of lights. People decorate their homes with lamps and candles. They wear new clothes, exchange sweets and celebrate the festival with their family and friends.

ONAM - Harvest Festival of Kerala

Onam is one of the most important festivals celebrated in Kerala. It is a harvest festival and is celebrated with great enthusiasm.

Legend of King Mahabali

Onam is traditionally associated with King Mahabali. According to popular belief, Mahabali was a generous and beloved king. Onam celebrates his annual return to Kerala.

Important Activities

Making Pookalam
Preparing Onam Sadhya
Vallam Kali or Snake Boat Races
Traditional Dances
Wearing Traditional Clothes
Family Gatherings

Onam Sadhya

Onam Sadhya is a traditional vegetarian feast served on a banana leaf. It includes several dishes such as rice, sambar, avial, thoran, olan, pickles, payasam and other traditional Kerala dishes.
